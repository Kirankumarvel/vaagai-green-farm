The story behind the farm

Three generations. One family.
One piece of ground.

His grandfather worked this land before there was a tractor on it. His father took over next, with better tools and the same soil under his feet. Then it was his turn. He had a B.E. Mechanical degree in hand and an engineering career ahead of him.

He chose the farm anyway. He says he did not leave engineering to become a farmer. He came back to being one. The degree is framed on a wall. The land is what he works every morning.

Vaagai Green Farm as a name started in 2020. The rule from day one has not changed: grow food we would feed our own family. No chemical sprays. No shortcuts. No plans to turn this into something it was never meant to be.

Every vegetable we sell is grown on this ground. Every basket is packed by hand before it leaves.

“Three generations worked this soil before me. I am not going to be the one who spoils it.”

Aravindan M

Founder of Vaagai Green Farm
Proud Mech | B.E. Mechanical Graduate

What We Stand For

Three things that guide every decision on this farm. No posters on the wall. Just the way we work.

Grown on our own land

No rented plots. No contract farming. No reselling. If it is not from this farm, we do not sell it. That is the whole policy.

No shortcuts

We do not use chemical sprays, ripening chemicals, cold storage, or waxing. We do not stretch shelf life with tricks. Vegetables are what they are.

Honest about what we are

We are not certified organic. We do not run workshops. We are a family farm doing one thing well. We would rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise.

How We Got Here

The short version of a long family history.

First generation

Grandfather

He worked this ground before machinery arrived. Bullock carts, hand watering, whatever the season gave. The land has been in the family since then.

Second generation

Father

Better tools, a tractor, larger yields. The farm kept going, kept the family fed, kept the soil worked. The name Vaagai had not arrived yet, but the work had.

2020

Vaagai Green Farm begins

The third generation takes over. Aravindan, B.E. Mechanical graduate leaves his engineering career to work the family land full-time. The farm gets a name and one rule: no chemical sprays.

Today

Vegetables, honey, and 100+ families

We grow leafy greens, seasonal vegetables and keep native bee colonies. More than 100 households around Arakkonam buy from us regularly. Same soil. Same rule.

What We Never Do

  • No synthetic pesticides. Not even once.
  • No chemical fertilisers on any bed.
  • No ripening chemicals or carbide.
  • No cold storage to stretch shelf life.
  • No middlemen or wholesale resale.
  • No waxing, no artificial shine on the vegetables.

Questions About the Farm

Who runs Vaagai Green Farm?
A third-generation farmer from Arakkonam. He trained in mechanical engineering (B.E. Mechanical) and left that career to work his family's land full-time. His grandfather farmed, his father farmed, and he is the third in line.
How long has the family been farming this land?
Three generations. The current farmer's grandfather worked this ground first, then his father, and now him. Vaagai Green Farm as a named operation started in 2020.
Why did the farmer leave engineering?
He wanted to work the family land himself rather than see it managed by someone else. He describes it as coming back to something, not running away from anything.
Do you farm on your own land?
Yes. Vaagai Green Farm operates on its own family farm land in Arakkonam. We are not a collective or a rented plot, which lets us control every step from sowing to harvest.
Is Vaagai Green Farm completely natural?
Yes. We have used no synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilisers since we started in 2020. We are not certified organic, because the certification cost is hard to justify for a farm our size, but the growing methods we follow are just as strict.
Where do you deliver?
We deliver across Arakkonam and nearby towns in Ranipet and Kanchipuram districts. Orders placed before 6 pm are usually delivered the next morning.
Can I visit the farm?
Yes, by appointment. Call ahead on +91 96551 18268 and we will set a time, usually on a weekend.
Do you run farming workshops?
Not yet. We plan to run short sessions on hydroponics, aquaponics and beekeeping in the future. When that starts, we will announce it on our social pages first.
Do you offer a weekly subscription basket?
Yes. Many families take a weekly basket of seasonal vegetables, either fixed or let us choose what is freshest that week. Message us on WhatsApp and we will set up a standing order.
Do you supply hotels and restaurants?
Yes. We supply a small number of kitchens in Arakkonam and Ranipet on weekly contracts. Send us your weekly requirement and we will tell you what we can commit to.
