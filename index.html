What Comes Off Our Farm

Four things we do on our own land in Arakkonam. Everything we sell, we grow ourselves.

Farm-Fresh Vegetables

Leafy greens, tomatoes, brinjal and seasonal vegetables. Picked in the morning, on your table the same day.

Beekeeping

Native bee colonies live on the farm. They pollinate the crops and give us raw honey we bottle ourselves.

Hydroponics

Some of our greens grow in water, not soil. It cuts water use by a wide margin and works through the year.

Aquaponics

Fish and plants share one system. The fish feed the plants, the plants clean the water, nothing goes to waste.

The story behind the farm

He could have stayed in the office.
He came back to the land.

Three generations of this family have worked the same ground in Arakkonam. His grandfather did it with bullock carts. His father did it with a tractor. He did it with a B.E. Mechanical degree in his hand and an engineering job waiting for him.

He chose the farm. The office could wait. The soil could not.

Vaagai Green Farm started in 2020 with one rule: grow food we would feed our own family. No sprays, no shortcuts, no plans to turn it into something it was never meant to be.

Questions We Get Asked

Is Vaagai Green Farm completely natural?
Yes. We have used no synthetic pesticides or chemical fertilisers since we started in 2020. We are not certified organic, because the certification cost is hard to justify for a farm our size, but the growing methods we follow are just as strict.
Who runs Vaagai Green Farm?
A third-generation farmer from Arakkonam. He trained in mechanical engineering (B.E. Mechanical) and left that career to work his family's land full-time. His grandfather farmed, his father farmed, and he is the third in line.
Do you farm on your own land?
Yes. Vaagai Green Farm operates on its own family farm land in Arakkonam. We are not a collective or a rented plot, which lets us control every step from sowing to harvest.
Where do you deliver?
We deliver across Arakkonam and nearby towns in Ranipet and Kanchipuram districts. Orders placed before 6 pm are usually delivered the next morning.
How much do the vegetables cost?
Prices move with the season and the crop. A weekly basket starts at around ₹400. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 96551 18268 for the current list and rates.
Can I visit the farm?
Yes, by appointment. Call ahead so someone is free to walk you around. Book a farm visit.
Do you run farming workshops?
Not yet. We plan to run short sessions on hydroponics, aquaponics and beekeeping in the future. When that starts, we will announce it on our social pages first.
What crops do you grow through the year?
Our year splits into three seasons. Summer gives us greens, brinjal, okra and tomatoes. The monsoon brings beans, gourds and leafy vegetables. Winter is for cabbage, cauliflower, carrot and beetroot. We plant what suits the season, not what sells fastest.
How do you handle pests without chemicals?
Neem oil, garlic and chilli sprays, and physical traps. We also plant marigolds and other border crops to draw pests away from the main beds and to bring in bees and ladybirds that feed on them. Healthy soil means stronger plants that fight off most damage on their own.
Is your produce certified organic?
No. We follow organic methods but do not carry a certification label. Certification costs more than a small farm can justify, and we would rather put that money into the soil and the seed. You are welcome to visit the farm and see how we grow before you buy.
What is the smallest order you deliver?
There is no strict minimum. Most households start with a weekly basket around ₹400 to ₹600. If you are close to Arakkonam, we can also do smaller orders on request.
Do you offer a weekly subscription basket?
Yes. Many families take a weekly basket of seasonal vegetables, either fixed or let us choose what is freshest that week. Message us on WhatsApp and we will set up a standing order.
Can I buy directly at the farm?
Yes, by appointment. Call ahead so we are free when you arrive, and you can pick up what is in season that day directly from the field.
What kind of honey do you sell?
Raw, unheated honey from native bee colonies kept on the farm. It is not blended, flavoured or pasteurised. What the bees made is what goes in the bottle.
Do you supply hotels and restaurants?
Yes. We supply a small number of kitchens in Arakkonam and Ranipet on weekly contracts. Send us your weekly requirement and we will tell you what we can commit to.
