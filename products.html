How We Farm

We are often asked whether we sell hydroponic setups or aquaponic systems. We do not. These are the methods we use on our own farm to grow the food we sell. They are not products or services for sale.

  • Natural growing

    We plant in soil, use compost from the farm, and rotate crops so the ground stays healthy. No synthetic fertilisers. Soil and water tested every season.

  • Hydroponics

    Some of our leafy greens grow in water instead of soil. It cuts water use by a wide margin and works through the year, even when the ground is resting.

  • Aquaponics

    Fish and plants share one closed water loop. The fish feed the plants, the plants clean the water. Nothing is wasted, nothing is dumped.

  • Natural pest control

    Neem oil, garlic and chilli sprays, and physical traps. Marigolds and border crops draw pests away from the main beds and bring in bees and ladybirds that feed on them.

  • Beekeeping

    Native bee colonies live on the farm. They pollinate the crops through the year, and give us honey we bottle by hand. Their presence is part of how the farm works.

  • Composting

    Crop waste, leaves and kitchen scraps from the farm go back into compost. What the soil gives us, we give back to it. Nothing leaves as waste.

Questions Before You Order

How do I order vegetables?
Message us on WhatsApp at +91 96551 18268 with what you need. Orders placed before 6 pm are usually delivered the next morning across Arakkonam and nearby towns.
What vegetables do you grow?
It changes with the season. Summer gives us greens, brinjal, okra and tomatoes. The monsoon brings beans, gourds and leafy vegetables. Winter is for cabbage, cauliflower, carrot and beetroot. We plant what suits the season, not what sells fastest.
Is there a minimum order?
There is no strict minimum. Most households start with a weekly basket around ₹400 to ₹600. If you are close to Arakkonam, we can also do smaller orders on request.
Do you offer a weekly subscription basket?
Yes. Many families take a weekly basket of seasonal vegetables, either fixed or let us choose what is freshest that week. Message us on WhatsApp and we will set up a standing order.
What is special about your honey?
It comes from native bee colonies kept on our own farm. It is single-origin, never heated, never blended. What the bees made is what goes in the bottle. Availability depends on the season.
Do you sell hydroponic or aquaponic systems?
No. We use hydroponics and aquaponics on our own farm to grow some of our greens, but we do not sell or install systems for others.
Do you run farming workshops?
Not yet. We plan to run short sessions on hydroponics, aquaponics and beekeeping in the future. When that starts, we will announce it on our social pages first.
Do you deliver outside Arakkonam?
Yes. We deliver across Arakkonam and nearby towns in Ranipet and Kanchipuram districts. For larger orders we can also arrange delivery to Vellore and Kanchipuram.
Can I buy directly at the farm?
Yes, by appointment. Call ahead on +91 96551 18268 so we are free when you arrive, and you can pick up what is in season that day.
Do you supply hotels and restaurants?
Yes. We supply a small number of kitchens in Arakkonam and Ranipet on weekly contracts. Send us your weekly requirement and we will tell you what we can commit to.
