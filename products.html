Explore Our Offerings

Farm-Fresh Vegetables

Tomatoes, greens, brinjal, okra and more, picked the same morning they reach you. No pesticides, no growth regulators. Weekly boxes for homes in and around Arakkonam.

Raw Honey & Pollination

Honey taken straight from our boxes and bottled without heating or blending. We also place hives on nearby farms during flowering season to help with pollination.

Hydroponic Systems

Soilless growing setups for terraces, schools, restaurants and small commercial units. A well-run system uses far less water than soil farming. Installation and training included.

Aquaponics Farming

Fish and plants growing in one loop. The fish waste feeds the plants and the plants clean the water. Two harvests from one system, almost nothing wasted.

Specialty Flowers

Seasonal flowers grown without chemical sprays. Available for functions, decorators and regular weekly orders.

Workshops & Training

Half-day and full-day sessions on composting, terrace gardening, hydroponics and beekeeping. We run them at the farm or at your campus.

Service FAQs

How do I order fresh vegetables from Vaagai Green Farm?

Call or message +91 96551 18268, or use the contact page. We take weekly orders and deliver within 24 hours across Arakkonam and nearby areas. You can order a mixed box or pick individual vegetables.

What does a farming workshop cost?

Workshops start at ₹500 per person. Full-day sessions and group bookings for schools, colleges and companies get a lower per-head rate. Ask us for a quote.

Do you deliver outside Arakkonam?

Our regular route covers Arakkonam and the surrounding towns. For larger orders we can arrange delivery to Ranipet, Kanchipuram, Vellore and parts of Chennai. Call us to check your area.

Can you set up a hydroponic system on my terrace?

Yes. We design and install hydroponic systems for terraces, balconies, schools and small commercial units. A typical home setup takes one to three days to install, and we train you on running it before we leave.

Is the honey really raw and unprocessed?

Yes. Our honey comes from our own bee boxes and is bottled without heating, blending or added sugar. It may crystallise over time, which is normal for raw honey.

Do you allow farm visits or school groups?

Yes. Farm visits are welcome by prior appointment. School and college groups can book a guided walk covering composting, beekeeping, hydroponics and aquaponics.

How long does a hydroponic setup take?

A small home or terrace unit is usually installed in one to three days. Larger commercial systems take longer, depending on size and site conditions. We confirm the timeline after a site visit.

Thinking of growing your own food?

Tell us about your space and we will suggest what will actually work there. No cost for the first conversation.
